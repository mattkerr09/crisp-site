What your PC needs

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, and graphics that support Vulkan. Most PCs from 2016 on have them. Vulkan is what Crisp's upscaler runs on, and there is no slower fallback that skips it, so it decides whether the AI part works on your PC.

If the driver offers no Vulkan, which happens on a basic display driver, inside a virtual machine or with a very old driver, the AI upscaler can’t run until a current graphics driver is installed, from your PC’s maker or from Intel, AMD or NVIDIA. Trimming, compressing and the other tools that don’t upscale don’t need Vulkan.

That is the whole of the difference: the editor, trimming and compressing still open and export, and the upscaler starts working once a current driver is installed. The free copy is the easiest way to find out before you pay.

The Windows installer is signed by Kerr & Company LLC. If the PC does not already have Microsoft’s WebView2 component, the installer downloads it from Microsoft while it installs. Crisp is also on the Microsoft Store.

Windows questions

Does Crisp work on Windows 10?

Yes, on 64-bit Windows 10 or Windows 11, with graphics that support Vulkan for the AI upscaler.

What happens if my PC has no Vulkan?

The AI upscaler can’t run until a current graphics driver with Vulkan is installed, from your PC’s maker or from Intel, AMD or NVIDIA. Trimming, compressing and the other tools that don’t upscale don’t need Vulkan, so they work in the meantime.

Is Max in the Windows app?

No. Max runs on Macs only. The Windows app has the same free limits and the same Pro price, without Max.

Can one key cover a Mac and a PC?

Yes. One key covers any three computers, Mac or Windows, at the same time.

Where do I enter my key on Windows?

Click Unlock Pro at the top right of Crisp and paste the key from your email, or choose Help > Enter Your Key… from the menu.
