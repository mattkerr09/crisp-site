How fast it runs on your macOS

Crisp is built for macOS 14 Sonoma and tested on macOS 14 and 26. Which upscaler it uses depends on your macOS:

  • macOS 15 Sequoia or later: Core ML, on your Mac's GPU. Full speed.
  • macOS 14 Sonoma: a different upscaler, Crisp's ncnn engine, also on your Mac's GPU. Upscaling takes about 1.5 times as long.

The picture comes out the same either way; only the wait changes.

Measured on a Mac Studio (M1 Ultra) in October 2026: a 3-second clip doubled to 720p took 3.4 seconds with Core ML and 5.0 seconds with the ncnn upscaler that macOS 14 uses. That is the macOS 14 upscaler timed on a newer macOS; it has not yet been timed on a Mac running macOS 14 itself.
