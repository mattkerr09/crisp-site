How big a video can you email? Gmail, Outlook, Mail Drop and WhatsApp limits

By Matt Kerr, who makes Crisp in Grand Rapids · published 2026-11-08 · last updated 2026-11-08
Quick answer

Gmail and Outlook.com both stop at 25 MB, which is about two minutes of 720p video. Apple's iCloud Mail Drop takes up to 5 GB. WhatsApp takes 100 MB sent as a video, or 2 GB sent as a document. I read each limit on the company's own help page on 8 November 2026. If the video runs longer than a couple of minutes, send a link instead of an attachment, or make the file smaller first.

How much video fits in 25 MB

A video's file size is its length times its bitrate, plus a little for the container. At about 1.6 Mbit/s, which is enough for 720p home video, 25 MB holds about two minutes. At about 0.8 Mbit/s, which suits an old tape transfer at 480p, it holds about four.

2 GB (WhatsApp document, OneDrive)1 h 22 min2 h 37 min5 h 21 min

MB here means 1,000,000 bytes, and I held back 4% for the container and for the encoder overshooting its target. The three bitrates are my working numbers for watchable home video, not a standard; busy scenes such as confetti or a windy tree need more.

One catch with email: an attachment travels encoded as text, which makes it about a third bigger on the way. Whether a 24 MB file still gets through depends on how the service counts, so for Gmail I aim for about 18 MB, or let Gmail make its Drive link.

What I measured on my Mac

I took a two-minute stretch of a 1938 home movie (Ivan Besse's reel from the Prelinger Archives, 640×480, 11.6 MB) and ran Crisp's Compress on it with H.264 output, on Crisp 1.0.1921, on 8 November 2026. Small file came out at 5.1 MB, 44% of the original, and Balanced at 6.3 MB, 54%. Either one goes through Gmail or Outlook.com as a plain attachment.

The whole 19-minute reel is 119 MB. On Small file it came out at 51.3 MB: still twice what an email takes, but under WhatsApp's 100 MB. That's the usual shape of it. Compression buys you a factor of two or three, and length decides the rest.

Sending it from a Mac

  1. Under about two minutes: make it smaller. In Crisp, drop the video in and type it's too big to email, or open Compress and pick Small file, Balanced or Best quality. Typing compress it to 720p also brings the picture size down, which saves the most. The smaller copy is saved next to your original, so check its size in Finder before you attach it. Compress runs at full length on the free copy, which adds a small “crispvideo.app” mark to video exports.
  2. Longer than that: send a link. In Apple Mail, Mail Drop takes up to 5 GB. Gmail makes a Drive link for you when the attachment is too big, and Outlook.com sends a OneDrive link.
  3. WhatsApp: send it as a document if you want the file itself to arrive, up to 2 GB. Sent as a video, the default limit is 100 MB at 720p.

Email size limits, in detail

Why did Gmail turn my video into a Drive link?

Because the attachments added up to more than 25 MB. Gmail takes the attachment off and puts a Google Drive link in the email instead, so the person opens the video from your Drive.

Is the Outlook.com limit 20 MB or 25 MB?

Microsoft's own Outlook.com page says the attachment size limit for files is 25 MB, as of 8 November 2026. Over that, share the file from OneDrive, which allows up to 2 GB.

How long does a Mail Drop attachment last?

Thirty days. Apple's page says the person has 30 days to save Mail Drop attachments after you send the email, and one message can carry up to 5 GB.

Does WhatsApp lower the quality of a video?

Sent as a video, the default limit is 100 MB at 720p, or 64 MB at 480p on a slower connection. Sent as a document, WhatsApp takes the file itself, up to 2 GB.

How long a video can I email?

About two minutes of 720p video fits in 25 MB, or about four minutes of 480p. Anything longer is easier to send as a Mail Drop, Drive or OneDrive link.
