The fastest way: just ask

Crisp has a plain-English box above the preview (“Tell Crisp what to do with the selected clip…”). Drop your video in and type what you want:

  • extract the audio: save it as an MP3
  • rip the audio (or pull the audio out)
  • save it as an mp3 / convert to mp3
  • just the audio: no video, sound only

Press Do it and Crisp saves the sound beside your original; the video on your timeline stays as it was. It's careful about intent, too: "extract the audio" saves the sound, while "get rid of the audio" mutes it instead: similar words, opposite jobs, and Crisp tells them apart.

The manual way: Save as ▸ Audio only

  1. Open Crisp and drop in your video

    Get the free Crisp app for Mac and drag your clip onto the window. Nothing is uploaded: the whole job runs on your Mac.

  2. Select the clip and pick a format

    Select the clip on your timeline. In the Tools panel, under Save as, pick a format: MP3, M4A (AAC), WAV, AIFF, FLAC, Apple Lossless, Opus, OGG or WMA.

  3. Press Audio only

    Crisp pulls the audio track out, encodes it on-device (a high-quality .mp3 unless you picked another format) and saves it beside your original. The clip on your timeline is left as it was.

No sound? Crisp says so

If the clip you dropped in has no audio track (a screen recording with sound off, a silent export) Crisp checks first and tells you plainly ("this clip has no audio track. There's nothing to extract") instead of handing you an empty file or a cryptic error. Small thing, but it's the difference between "it just works" and "why is this file 0 KB".

Pulling out the audio, in detail

How do I extract audio from a video on a Mac for free?

Use Crisp: drop your clip in, select it and press Audio only under Save as in the Tools panel (or type "extract the audio" and press Do it). It saves the sound as an MP3, or the format you pick, entirely on your Mac, with no account, no upload.

What format do I get?

An MP3 by default: universal, high quality, and it plays everywhere. Pick another in the Save as menu (M4A, WAV, AIFF, FLAC, Apple Lossless, Opus, OGG or WMA) or name it as you type: “save the audio as a wav”. Crisp writes it next to your original video.

Is my video uploaded to extract the sound?

No. The whole thing runs on your Mac. Nothing leaves the device, so there's no queue and no size cap.

What if there's no audio in the clip?

Crisp checks and tells you there's nothing to extract, rather than producing an empty file.
