How-to guides

By Matt Kerr, who makes Crisp in Grand Rapids · published 2026-08-26 · last updated 2026-11-11

Forty-three short guides for the things people actually need doing to a video on a Mac. Each one names the setting that matters and says what it costs you, rather than listing every control on the screen. Time is the cost that varies most: on my M1 Ultra a 60-second, two-clip edit exported at 1080p in 6.7 seconds, while upscaling a 40-second 1080p phone clip to 4K took 3 min 26 s (Crisp 1.0.1777, 2 November 2026).

Two things are true of all of them. Everything happens on your own machine, so your videos never leave your computer and nothing needs an account. And where a step is destructive (where doing it early makes a later step worse) the guide says so, because the order you work in matters more than the tool you pick.
