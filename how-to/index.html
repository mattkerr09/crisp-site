How-to guides

Forty-six short guides for the things people actually need doing to a video on a Mac. Each one names the setting that matters and says what it costs you, rather than listing every control on the screen.

Two things are true of all of them. Everything happens on your own machine, so nothing is uploaded and nothing needs an account. And where a step is destructive — where doing it early makes a later step worse — the guide says so, because the order you work in matters more than the tool you pick.
