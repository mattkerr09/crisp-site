How to compress a video for Discord

Quick answer

Discord lets free accounts send files up to 20 MB, Nitro Basic 50 MB and Nitro up to 1 GB, and it checks the size before it compresses anything. To get a longer clip under 20 MB, re-encode it as H.264 at a bitrate that lands just below the limit. In my test, Crisp took a 38.6 MB two-minute clip to 18.5 MB at 960×720 in 46 seconds, with a VMAF score of 92.9.

Discord's limits, as of today

AccountLargest file you can send
Free20 MB
Nitro Basic50 MB
NitroUp to 1 GB

Read on Discord's File Attachments FAQ on 10 November 2026. The free limit went up from 10 MB in August 2026, so pages that still say 10 MB or 8 MB are out of date.

Sources: Discord, File Attachments FAQ · Prelinger Archives, Ivan Besse home movies

Three details on that page matter more than the number:

  • The size counts before compression. Discord used to compress on mobile first and complain afterwards. Now desktop and mobile both check the file you pick, so the number you see is the one that counts.
  • Android gets caught more often. Discord says phones on Android tend to make larger source files, so they hit the limit more often than before, even though the limit went up.
  • Discord says it is experimenting with upload limits, and some accounts may be able to send more. If yours lets you send a bigger file, that's why.

Discord lists MP4 files encoded with H.264, HEVC or AV1. H.264 is the safe choice: it's the oldest of the three, and almost every phone and browser plays it.

How much video fits in 20 MB

A file's size is its length times its bitrate. Aim a little under the limit, because encoders overshoot. Crisp aims at 19 MB, which is 152 megabits, and gives the sound 64 kbps. Here's what's left for the picture:

LengthBitrate left for the picture
30 secondsabout 5,000 kbps
1 minuteabout 2,470 kbps
2 minutesabout 1,200 kbps
3 minutesabout 780 kbps
5 minutesabout 440 kbps
10 minutesabout 190 kbps

The arithmetic: 152,000 kilobits divided by the length in seconds, minus 64 for the sound. At two minutes, my test landed at 960×720. The longer the clip, the smaller the picture has to be, and past about fifteen minutes it doesn't fit at all: Crisp's floor is 100 kbps, the least it gives a 360p picture.

Step by step

  1. Open the video in Crisp, on a Mac or a Windows PC.
  2. Type what you want: "under 20 MB for Discord". Or use the Fit a size control in the Compress panel. If you have Nitro Basic, ask for 50 MB instead.
  3. Check the card. Crisp shows what it will do before it starts: "Fit to 20 MB with H.264, aimed at 19 MB to stay under Discord's 20 MB".
  4. Export. Crisp tries a picture size, checks the result, and steps down if it lands over the aim.
  5. Drag the file into Discord. It's an H.264 MP4, a format Discord lists.

Everything runs on your computer. The video isn't uploaded anywhere, which matters for clips of family or friends.

If you enhanced the clip first, as I did, fit it to size last. An upscale makes the file much bigger: Crisp's 4x Enhance turned the 11.6 MB clip into the 38.6 MB one. Discord only cares about the final size.

FAQ

What is Discord's file size limit in 2026?

20 MB a file on a free account, since August 2026. Nitro Basic allows 50 MB and Nitro up to 1 GB. Discord says it is experimenting with limits, so some accounts may see more.

Does Discord check the size before or after it compresses?

Before. Discord now checks the file you pick on both desktop and mobile, so a file over 20 MB is refused on a free account, wherever you send it from.

How long a video can I send on Discord for free?

It depends on the picture size. In my test a two-minute clip fitted in 20 MB at 960×720. Longer clips need a smaller picture, and past about fifteen minutes even 360p doesn't fit, so split it or send a link.

What video format works best on Discord?

An MP4 encoded with H.264. Discord lists H.264, HEVC and AV1, and H.264 is the one almost every phone and browser plays.

Can I compress a video for Discord without uploading it?

Yes. A desktop app such as Crisp does it on your own computer, and some browser tools say they keep the file in the browser. Read each tool's page before you trust it with a private clip.
