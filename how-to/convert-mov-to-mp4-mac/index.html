The fastest way: just ask

Crisp has a plain-English box above the preview (“Tell Crisp what to do with the selected clip…”). Drop your video in and type it:

  • convert to mp4: the usual one
  • make it an mp4: same thing
  • convert it to mov: the other way
  • change the format to mp4: plain English

Press Do it and Crisp writes the new file beside your original and puts it on your timeline.

Step by step

  1. Open Crisp and drop in your video

    Get the free Crisp app for Mac and drag your MOV onto the window. Every step runs on your Mac; your videos never leave it.

  2. Ask for the format

    Your clip is on the timeline, selected. Type “convert it to mp4” in the box above the preview.

  3. Name MP4 or MOV

    The format you name is the setting: MP4 or MOV. When the video already fits (H.264/HEVC, which most MOVs are), Crisp with Pro copies it losslessly: no re-encode, no quality loss, and it's fast. The free version runs the whole clip and adds its small “crispvideo.app” mark, which re-encodes the picture once.

  4. Press Do it

    The .mp4 lands beside your original, and your MOV is untouched. Exporting a whole edit instead? The Export panel's Container menu writes MP4, MOV or MKV.

Lossless with Pro when it can be: no needless re-encode

A MOV and an MP4 are just different wrappers around the same video. Most MOV files hold H.264 or HEVC, which live happily in an MP4, so Crisp with Pro does a stream copy: it re-wraps the exact same video (no re-encode, no quality loss, seconds not minutes). It only falls back to re-encoding if the source codec genuinely can't live in the target container. Want a smaller file too? That's Compress, a different job.

Convert vs Compress

  • Convert: change the format/container (MOV → MP4), keep the quality. That's this guide.
  • Compress (shrink the file size (for email/upload), keep the resolution) or name a smaller one (“make it 720p”). See compress a video.

MOV and MP4 questions

How do I convert MOV to MP4 on a Mac for free?

Use Crisp: drop your MOV in, type "convert to mp4" in the box above the preview and press Do it. It converts on your Mac: no account, no upload. Free exports run the full length and carry a small “crispvideo.app” mark.

Does it lose quality?

With Pro, usually not. The free version runs the whole clip and re-encodes once to add its “crispvideo.app” mark. Most MOVs hold H.264/HEVC, which Crisp copies straight into the MP4 (a lossless remux with Pro). It only re-encodes if the codec can't fit.

Can it do AVI / MKV / WebM?

Yes. Drop those in and convert to MP4 or MOV; lossless with Pro when the codec fits, else re-encoded. The free version runs the whole clip and re-encodes once to add its mark.

Convert or Compress?

Convert changes the format and, with Pro, keeps quality; Compress shrinks the file size. Different jobs.
