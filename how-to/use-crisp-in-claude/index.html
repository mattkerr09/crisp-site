How to add Crisp's free video tools to Claude and ChatGPT

By Matt Kerr, who makes Crisp in Grand Rapids · published 2026-11-09 · last updated 2026-11-11

Crisp Video Helper is free in Claude and ChatGPT. For Claude, follow the quick answer below. For ChatGPT, follow the custom server steps.

Quick answer

In Claude, open Customize, then Connectors. Click + Add, then Add custom connector. Name it Crisp, paste https://mcp.crispvideo.app/mcp as the server URL, click Continue, pick No sign in under Authentication, and click Add. In a chat, switch it on from the + button under Connectors. That is the path Claude's own help page gave on 9 November 2026 for the Free, Pro and Max plans; the Free plan allows one custom connector. After that you can ask Claude how to get a two-minute video under 20 MB for Discord, or what to set before you capture a VHS tape, and it works the answer out with Crisp's tools.

Add Crisp Video Helper to ChatGPT

Until Crisp's listing is approved in ChatGPT's app directory, add it as a custom server:

  1. Go to ChatGPT's Plugins page. Select the + button, then Add custom MCP server.
  2. Enter the name Crisp Video Helper and a short description, such as "Free help with old tapes, video faults, camcorder files and sending limits".
  3. Under Connection, enter https://mcp.crispvideo.app/mcp.
  4. Set authentication to none. The helper has no account.
  5. Review the risk warning. Select I understand and want to continue, then Create as a plugin.
  6. Start a new conversation. Type @ and select Crisp Video Helper.

Source: OpenAI's instructions for connecting a plugin to ChatGPT, read 11 November 2026.

Account and workspace policies apply. Some ChatGPT plans or work accounts may not allow custom servers. Once the listing is approved, Crisp Video Helper will be in ChatGPT's app directory. This page will say so.

What the 12 tools answer

All 12 tools are read-only. Ask Claude or ChatGPT in plain words and it picks the tool. These groups follow the server's own tool descriptions, read from tools/list on 11 November 2026.

  • Tapes: identify_tape suggests a format and player; plan_transfer lists equipment, steps and time; capture_settings gives picture and sound settings; storage_needed estimates disk space.
  • Files: identify_file suggests a format and player from a name or folder; convert_plan plans playback, editing or archiving and explains what is kept or lost; date_fix_command writes a command to put a confirmed recording date in a separate MP4 or MOV copy.
  • Fixing: diagnose_video suggests faults from symptoms and how to tell them apart; fix_steps puts free-tool steps in order and explains what cannot be recovered; ffmpeg_command writes one command for a separate local copy.
  • Sharing: share_settings gives export settings, dated or unchecked sending limits, a size estimate and a route for larger files; fit_to_size works out a bitrate and writes two ffmpeg passes, or suggests splitting the video or sharing a link.

Two real answers, from 10 November 2026

These are excerpts from the helper's answers in a live run on 10 November 2026.

A cassette labelled Hi8 120. The identify_tape tool answered:

Likely format: Hi8.
A Hi8 camcorder, or a Digital8 camcorder that explicitly supports analog tape playback.
A Video8-only player usually cannot play a Hi8 recording properly.
Facts checked against named sources.

A two-minute video for Discord. The fit_to_size tool answered:

MB means 1,000,000 bytes. All rates below are in decimal kbit/s.
20 MB = 20000000 bytes. Hold back 4% for the container and rate-control overshoot.
Total = 20000000 x 8 x 0.96 / 120 / 1000 = 1280 kbit/s.
AAC stereo = 128 kbit/s.
Video = 1280 - 128 = 1152 kbit/s.
discord: Free uploads allow 20 MB, Nitro Basic 50 MB, and Nitro up to 1 GB. On desktop and mobile, the size counts before Discord compresses anything. Limit checked: 2026-11-09.
Chosen height: 480p. The video rate written in the commands is 1152 kbit/s.

Advice only. This server has not opened a file or run these commands.
Not yet checked, so treat as estimates: share (floors.1080, floors.720, floors.480, floors.360).

Earlier examples, from 9 November 2026

A two-minute clip for Discord. Discord's own help page says the free upload limit has been 20 MB since August 2026, up from 10 MB, and that it checks the size before any compression. I asked the connector's fit_to_size tool for a two-minute video under 20 MB. It counted 20 MB as 20,000,000 bytes, held back 4% for the container and for the encoder overshooting, and came to 1,280 kbit/s in total. Take 128 kbit/s for stereo AAC sound and 1,152 kbit/s is left for the picture, so it chose 480p rather than starve a 1080p frame, then wrote the two ffmpeg passes. It also said which of its own floors it hasn't checked yet, so you know which number is a planning figure.

A VHS tape on a Mac. For an NTSC VHS tape, capture_settings answered: 720×480 at 29.97 frames a second, interlaced; keep the interlacing at capture and don't force a field order; ProRes 422 or a lossless codec such as FFV1; audio at 48 kHz; S-Video if the VCR has it, composite if not. It also says that the capture size is not the detail on the tape, which is the line most guides leave out. Then storage_needed put ten hours of FFV1 at about 187 to 457 GB. The range is wide on purpose: FFV1 is lossless, so tape noise decides the size, and the tool labels its 40 to 100 Mbit/s rate as a planning figure rather than a measured one.

The whole transfer, from the VCR and the capture box to the cleaned-up file, is in how to convert VHS to digital on a Mac.

It never sees a video

The helper gives advice only. Claude or ChatGPT sends it text: a running time, the words on a cassette label, a symptom. It opens no files and runs no commands. It keeps nothing it is sent and writes no log of inputs. The only stored record is the current UTC day and call count. A short-lived counter per session limits rapid calls.

The commands it writes run on your own computer, if you choose to run them. They save a separate copy and leave the original alone. How the helper handles data.

Claude's help page also explains where the connection comes from: Anthropic's servers reach the connector over the internet, not your computer. That's why it works the same in the browser, the desktop app and the phone app, and why it can't touch your files. The server answers up to 20,000 calls a day in total, shared by everyone, which keeps it free without an account.

On a Team or Enterprise plan, an Owner adds it first, under Organization settings, then Connectors, and everyone else connects to it from their own Connectors list.

When you need the app instead

The connector can tell you a tape is a Hi8, that the stripes on moving edges are interlacing, or that the hiss is tape noise. It can't take them out. That's what the Crisp app is for: it deinterlaces, denoises, steadies and upscales the video on your own Mac or Windows PC, offline, and your videos never leave your computer. It's free to use, with upscaling, restoring and colour exports stopping at 15 seconds until you buy Pro.

Questions people ask

Is the Crisp connector free?

Yes. There is no account, no sign-in and no key. Pick "No sign in" when Claude asks about authentication.

Can it see or upload my videos?

No. It only gets the text Claude or ChatGPT sends it. Your files stay on your computer.

Does it work on the free Claude plan?

Claude's help page says custom connectors work on the Free plan, limited to one custom connector, and on Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise. Read on 9 November 2026.

Can I add Crisp Video Helper to ChatGPT now?

Yes, as a custom server if your account and workspace policies allow it. Follow the ChatGPT steps above. Once the listing is approved, it will be in ChatGPT's app directory and this page will say so.

Why does an answer say a figure is "not yet checked"?

Because some numbers are planning floors rather than limits read on a company's own page. The connector labels each one, so you can tell a dated limit (Gmail's 25 MB) from an estimate (the bitrate a 480p picture needs).
