Quick answer

That measures grain cleanup, not damaged-print repair or rival output. Crisp's photo tools are Mac only; on Windows it upscales video, not photos.

Crisp: the grain result and its limits

The grain guide published 11 October 2026 measured Crisp 1.0.1460 on an M1 Ultra Mac Studio. Grain was added to photos with known clean originals, so the test could compare each output with the original. This is our controlled grain test, not a comparison against the apps in this table.

Add a photo to the timeline, type fix the grain in this photo and press Do it. The request used Denoise 0.6 and landed 0.4 to 2.9 dB closer to the clean original than the Upscale photo button. The default button could still look smoother to the eye, especially with heavy grain. Read the full measurement and crops before treating the score as a promise.

The Old photos panel offers Clean, Enlarge 2x, Enlarge 4x for printing, and Sepia. Clean denoises and sharpens at the photo's own size. The grain request does not measure the Old photos panel or damaged prints. Your videos and photos never leave your computer.

Crisp cannot remove scratches or tears, colourise photos, restore faces, or automatically repair faded colour. Those need another tool. Dust removal is a video option only.

Match the change to the picture

A small but clean photo may only need enlargement. A grainy scan needs texture cleanup before you decide whether to print larger. A torn print needs a damage-repair workflow. Split a folder by those needs rather than running one change across every image.

For a real family photo you rarely have a clean reference. Inspect skin texture and fine lines yourself, and keep the original scan. Use the old photo steps for a folder or the image enlargement comparison when size is the only issue.

Questions about this comparison

Is enlarging a picture the same as removing its grain?

No. Enlargement changes output dimensions. Grain cleanup changes texture. The two Crisp routes in the measurement gave different results, so choose the change your picture needs.

Can I treat a photo enhancer score as a promise for my album?

No. The grain score used known clean originals and added grain. Your scans have different damage and usually no clean reference. Inspect a copy before treating more pictures.

Does Clean in the Old photos panel rebuild a damaged face?

No. Clean denoises and sharpens at the original photo size. Crisp does not offer face repair, scratch repair or colourisation for photos.

Should I use the same fix for grain and a faded print?

No. Crisp can reduce grain but cannot automatically repair faded colour. Keep the scan for a separate colour workflow.
