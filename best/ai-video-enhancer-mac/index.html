Questions about this comparison

Can an enhancer make a face look sharper but less accurate?

Yes. A model can draw plausible marks that were never recorded. Compare a face against the source and keep the original as the record of the event.

Are the clip results here scores for all the video tools?

No. The measured result belongs to Crisp on the selected source clips. We have not run the same footage through every listed option.

Does every Mac video model run on the Mac itself?

No. Check the local-or-upload column and any model-specific limits. A Mac download can also offer cloud features.

What should I put in an enhancement sample?

Use a moving face, a pan and fine repeating texture when available. Play the result as well as pausing it, because changing texture between frames may be missed in a still image.
