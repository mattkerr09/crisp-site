Topaz Video: model choice and cloud rendering

Topaz has cloud rendering and a long track record. Its deeper model library is useful if you want to choose the model for each shot. Starlight Mini runs locally; Astra holds the cloud-only Starlight models. Crisp's Max restore runs on the Mac itself. Topaz's trim and crop controls do not arrange clips on a timeline. Features read 19 October 2026 in the Topaz comparison.

Adobe completed its purchase of Topaz Labs on 23 September 2026 (Adobe, read 20 Nov 2026); Topaz Video is still sold on its own.

Aiarty: choose the model for the damage

Aiarty offers several restoration models for damage such as compression, low resolution and soft focus. If you want that control per clip, it gives you more choice than Crisp. It also has a longer Windows record. It lists trim and crop rather than a timeline for arranging clips. Features read 19 October 2026; see the Aiarty comparison.

HitPaw VikPea: models named for the job

HitPaw makes model selection clear with face, animation and general models. That can be a better fit when your footage is one clear kind. It also offers paid cloud rendering. Crisp makes an automatic choice and processes your footage on your computer. HitPaw's free version previews results but does not export. Features read 19 October 2026; see the HitPaw comparison.

VideoProc: formats and DVDs

VideoProc is stronger for format conversion, DVD ripping and large conversion queues. If your main job is turning a shelf of discs or camcorder files into usable files, choose it for that. Its cut and merge tools work in a queue. Digiarty's timeline editor, VideoProc Vlogger, is a separate product. Features read 19 October 2026; see the VideoProc comparison.

AI video enhancer questions

Which AI video enhancer is best for my Mac?

Choose by the work. Crisp combines restoration and a timeline edit. For more model choice, compare the rival tools above. Try your own footage before buying. This page does not claim a shared quality test across all five tools.

Can I export an enhanced sample with Crisp for free?

Yes. Free enhanced exports stop at 15 seconds and carry a small "crispvideo.app" mark. Pro removes the limit and mark. Trim to the moment you want to judge first.

Does Crisp work on Windows too?

Yes. Video restoration and the timeline editor run on Mac and PC. Max restore runs on Mac today.

Can AI recover detail that was never recorded?

No. A model can draw plausible detail. That can change a face or put marks on a sign. Keep the source and compare the result before sharing it as a record of what happened.
