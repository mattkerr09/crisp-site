TODO-DE: What the 15 seconds means in practice

TODO-DE: The limit is per export, not per day. Upscale a 2-minute clip on the free copy and you get its first 15 seconds back, enlarged, with the mark, and the app tells you what was cut. Before / After shows the same moment, so you can judge the result on your own footage before you pay anything.

TODO-DE: To spend the 15 seconds on the part you care about, trim the clip to it first, then run Enhance on it. Anything that is not AI work, such as converting a MOV to MP4 or compressing a file to email it, runs at full length with no limit.

TODO-DE: What Pro adds besides removing the limits

TODO-DE: Max. TODO-DE: A generative restore for old, soft footage, on Macs only. It is slow on purpose: on my M1 Ultra Mac Studio it took about 31 seconds a frame, so a 4-second clip takes most of an hour.

TODO-DE: Batch. TODO-DE: Run the same job on many files in one go.

TODO-DE: Every 1.x update is included. There is no account to make and nothing to renew.

TODO-DE: Free vs Pro questions

TODO-DE: Is the free version a trial?

TODO-DE: No. There is no clock and no sign-up. The free copy keeps working with the same two limits for as long as you use it.

TODO-DE: Does the free copy put a mark on photos?

TODO-DE: No. The mark goes on video and GIF exports only. An upscaled or cleaned-up photo from the free copy is full size and unmarked.

TODO-DE: How many computers does Pro cover?

TODO-DE: Up to three at a time, Mac or Windows, on one key. After you activate a computer once, Crisp works offline on it.

TODO-DE: Is Max in the Windows app?

TODO-DE: No. Max runs on Macs only. The Windows app has the same free limits and the same Pro price, without Max.

TODO-DE: What if Pro is not what I wanted?

TODO-DE: Ask for a refund within 30 days and you get it, no reason needed.
