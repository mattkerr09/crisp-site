Where UniFab wins

  • 16K output. UniFab's desktop Video Upscaler AI reaches 16K. Crisp does not write 16K video.
  • More kinds of enhancement. UniFab All-In-One includes SDR to HDR, colourising and face enhancement. Crisp does none of those.
  • More computers on the suite licence. UniFab All-In-One covers 5 computers. Crisp covers up to 3 at a time.
  • Watermark-free trials. Video Upscaler AI has a 30-day trial with full access and no watermark. All-In-One allows three trials per feature, with no watermark. Crisp's free version marks AI exports and stops them at 15 seconds, but it never expires.
  • Intel Macs. UniFab's desktop upscaler supports Intel and Apple Silicon Macs. Crisp's Mac app needs Apple Silicon.

Local desktop work or a cloud upload?

The redirect matters: the page you land on is an online enhancer that uploads video to UniFab's cloud. It offers up to 4K with 2x enlargement and says files are deleted within 3 days.

UniFab's desktop Video Upscaler AI can instead run on your own GPU, or use FabCloud credits for cloud processing. It would be wrong to say that every UniFab job uploads your video. Crisp processes your footage on your own computer, with no upload. After the key activates once, Crisp works fully offline.

Who should pick which?

  • Pick Crisp if you want a lower one-time price, local processing and a real editor in the same app. One licence covers up to 3 Mac or Windows computers at a time.
  • Pick UniFab Video Upscaler AI if you need 16K output, Intel Mac support or its 30-day watermark-free trial.
  • Pick UniFab All-In-One if you need SDR to HDR, colourising, face enhancement or its 5-computer licence. The upscaler alone and the suite are different purchases.

We have not measured Crisp and UniFab on the same footage. Use your own clip to judge the picture, and check the current price on the linked vendor page before buying.

Edit first, upscale the whole cut

Crisp has a timeline, trimming and colour grading in the same app. Cut your clips together, then apply the upscale to the whole edit in one export. Presets include Auto, Old home video, Enhance to 4K, Low-light and Anime / Cartoon. Max quality is also available on the Mac.

What 16x means in Crisp

The editor's Scale menu offers 2x, 3x, 4x, 8x and 16x. The 8x and 16x options use two passes: 4x followed by 2x or 4x. The second pass adds pixels, not detail. It does not make the picture four times better.

Output is capped at about 80 megapixels per frame. That makes 16x useful for very small sources, up to about 640x480. A 1080p clip reaches 7680x4320, or 8K, at 4x. In a measured run on 24 November 2026, Crisp 1.0.2193 upscaled a 160x120 clip 16x to 2560x1920 in 8 seconds on an M1 Ultra. That is one small clip on one Mac, not a speed promise for other footage.

Questions people ask

What happened to DVDFab Enlarger AI's page?

DVDFab's Enlarger AI page redirects to UniFab's online AI video enhancer at https://unifab.ai/ai-video-enhancer-online.htm. DVDFab now sends Enlarger AI visitors to UniFab.

Does UniFab always upload my video?

No. UniFab Video Upscaler AI for desktop can run on your own GPU or in the cloud with FabCloud credits. The online enhancer uploads your video to UniFab cloud and says files are deleted within 3 days. Crisp upscales on your computer and uploads no video.

Does Crisp offer 16K, colourising, SDR to HDR or face enhancement?

No. Crisp does not write 16K video, colourise black-and-white video, convert SDR to HDR or enhance faces with a face model. UniFab All-In-One includes those features.

Is the UniFab upscaler the same purchase as All-In-One?

No. The Mac Video Upscaler AI Lifetime price is US$84.99, with a US$129.99 list price. Its FabCloud version is US$103.99 lifetime. UniFab All-In-One Lifetime is US$219.99 now, with a US$319.99 standard price and a licence for 5 computers. These are US dollar prices read on 24 November 2026.
