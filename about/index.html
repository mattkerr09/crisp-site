What Crisp is

Crisp Video restores, denoises and upscales old or low-resolution video to 4K, then lets you edit it on a timeline. It runs on the Mac itself: your video is not uploaded anywhere. It needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later) on macOS 26.0 or later. The upscaling guide shows how it works, step by step.
