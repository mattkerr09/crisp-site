Who makes it

I'm Matthew Kerr. I build Crisp myself in Grand Rapids, Michigan, and I sell it under Kerr & Company LLC, a small independent software company registered in Michigan. There is no team behind the name: you are buying from the person who made it.

What Crisp is

Crisp Video restores, denoises and upscales old or low-resolution video to 4K, then lets you edit it on a timeline. It runs on the Mac itself: your video is not uploaded anywhere. It needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later) on macOS 14 or later. The upscaling guide shows how it works, step by step.
